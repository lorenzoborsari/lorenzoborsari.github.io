Profile

Lorenzo’s path begins with a law degree at the Alma Mater Studiorum in Bologna and continues with courtroom practice at Studio Daniele, where he qualified as a trial substitute. Since October 2025 he has put that training at the service of San Marino Innovation, providing legal counsel within the Sammarinese innovation ecosystem.

Alongside law, he has long pursued a second, editorial vocation: after professional training in editing and copywriting at La Scuola di Editoria, he works as a freelance editor for several publishing houses and literary agencies, also handling copyright matters and archive digitisation.

A third thread leads to the classroom: between 2022 and 2024 he taught Law and Civic Education, History, Geography and Religion in primary and secondary schools in Bologna. Today he keeps studying, between the SPISA postgraduate diploma in Public Administration Sciences and an IFOA programme on sustainability reporting and ESG/CSRD regulation.
